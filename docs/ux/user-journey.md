# User Journey

> User journey for the goal: **purchase flowers for a birthday using AI assistance.**


## Step 1 — Major Customer Goal

Customer wants to purchase flowers for a birthday using AI assistance.

## Step 2 — Persona, Scenario, Goal

**Persona:** Guest / New Customer

**Scenario:** A customer needs to purchase flowers for a birthday and uses the website, including the AI assistant, to help choose an appropriate arrangement.

**Goal:** Find, customize, and purchase a suitable birthday arrangement with the desired delivery option, then receive confirmation.

## Step 3 — Happy Path
1.  Home
2.  Browse / Search arrangements
3.  AI recommends a suitable birthday arrangement
4.  Customer selects product
5.  Customize arrangement
6.  Add to cart
7.  Review cart
8.  Choose delivery date/time
9.  Enter recipient/order details
10. Payment
11. Order confirmation

## Step 4 — Alternative Paths

### Alternative 1 — Product unavailable
1. Customer selects a recommended arrangement
2. Product unavailable
3. AI suggests an available alternative
4. Customer selects alternative
5. Continue to customization/cart

### Alternative 2 — Delivery slot unavailable
1. Customer chooses delivery
2. Preferred date/time unavailable
3. System shows available slots
4. Customer selects another slot
5. Continue to checkout

### Alternative 3 — Customer changes the recommendation
1. AI adds recommended item to cart
2. Customer reviews cart
3. Customer changes or removes item
4. Continue with revised cart

## Step 5 — Failure Scenarios

### Failure 1 — Payment fails
1. Customer submits payment
2. Payment fails
3. Clear payment-error message
4. Customer retries or chooses another payment method
5. Payment succeeds
6. Order confirmation

### Failure 2 — Order/payment status cannot be confirmed
1. Payment attempt occurs
2. Status is uncertain
3. System does not incorrectly confirm the order
4. Order/payment status is reconciled accurately
5. Customer receives the correct confirmation/status

### Failure 3 — Delivery fails
1. Order is out for delivery
2. Delivery fails
3. Staff records the reason
4. Customer is contacted
5. Redelivery or pickup is arranged

## AI Interaction Points

AI recommends a suitable birthday arrangement, can add recommendations to the cart, and can suggest an available alternative when a selected product is unavailable. The customer remains responsible for reviewing the cart and completing checkout and payment.
