# Functional Requirements

The following functional requirements translate the documented User Stories and Lily’s interview findings into specific, testable system requirements.

## FR-01 — Product Catalog

The system shall allow customers to browse products and view photos, prices, sizes, flower types, colors, and occasions.

## FR-02 — Product Customization

The system shall allow customers to customize eligible arrangements by selecting available size, colors, message, and extras before adding them to the cart.

## FR-03 — Product Availability

The system shall display current product availability and prevent customers from ordering products that are unavailable.

## FR-04 — Delivery and Pickup Scheduling

The system shall allow customers to select available delivery or pickup dates and time slots, including same-day delivery when available and requested by the 1 PM cutoff.

## FR-05 — Checkout

The system shall allow customers to complete an order as a guest or through an account.

## FR-06 — Order Review

The system shall allow customers to review and change order details before payment.

## FR-07 — Payment Methods

The system shall support card payments, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

## FR-08 — Payment Failure Handling

The system shall display a clear message when payment fails and allow the customer to retry or use another supported payment method.

## FR-09 — Confirmation and Order Status

The system shall provide confirmation after successful payment and allow customers to view order status and receive status updates.

## FR-10 — Customer Account and Loyalty

The system shall allow returning customers to save recipient details, addresses, and favorites; view previous orders and reorder; and earn, view, and redeem loyalty rewards.

## FR-11 — AI Information Support

The system shall allow the AI assistant to answer questions about flowers, arrangements, prices, care, occasions, availability, delivery, and promotions.

## FR-12 — AI Recommendations

The system shall allow the AI assistant to recommend arrangements and complementary products based on the customer's occasion, budget, colors, and available preferences.

## FR-13 — AI Cart Addition

The system shall allow the AI assistant to add recommended products to the customer's cart and clearly identify the items it added.

## FR-14 — Customer Control of AI Cart Items

The system shall allow customers to review, change, or remove AI-added items before checkout, and shall prevent the AI assistant from placing the order or completing payment.

## FR-15 — AI Language Support

The system shall allow customers to communicate with the AI assistant in English or Irish while using the same product, pricing, availability, and delivery information.

## FR-16 — AI Escalation

The system shall allow the AI assistant to escalate uncertain, unusual, custom, or complaint-related requests to staff.

## FR-17 — Staff Order Management

The system shall allow authorized staff to view and update order statuses, delivery or pickup information, and relevant customer details.

## FR-18 — Inventory Management

The system shall allow authorized staff to view inventory quantities and low-stock or unavailable items and record quantities received, used, damaged, or unavailable.

FR-19 — Product and Price Management

The system shall allow authorized managers to create and manage arrangements and prices without technical assistance.

FR-20 — Promotion Management

The system shall allow authorized managers to create, schedule, edit, and end promotions without technical assistance.

FR-21 — Seasonal Content and Holiday Limits

The system shall allow authorized managers to manage seasonal content and holiday order limits without technical assistance.

## FR-22 — Subscriptions

The system shall allow customers to subscribe to recurring flower deliveries with a preferred schedule, duration, size, and style.

## FR-23 — Gift Cards

The system shall allow customers to purchase and redeem digital gift cards.

## FR-24 — Event Inquiries

The system shall allow wedding or event customers to submit event date, venue, guest count, floral needs, budget, style, and inspiration photos for Lily's review.

## FR-25 — Human Support

The system shall allow customers to submit human-support inquiries through available support channels and allow staff to record and track those inquiries.

## FR-26 — Delivery Management

The system shall allow authorized staff to manage delivery capacity and record failed-delivery outcomes so customers can be contacted for redelivery or pickup.

## FR-27 — Management Reporting

The system shall allow authorized managers to view reports covering sales, order volume, busy periods, popular arrangements, inventory, deliveries, and loyalty activity.

## Traceability Coverage

FR-01–FR-27 and NFR-01–NFR-08 provide coverage for the documented US-01–US-30 User Stories. The eight carried-forward open questions remain unresolved and are not assigned invented values.
